Hall of Shame: Winning With One QB

Hall of Shame: Winning With One QB

This is a 2-QB league. The whole point of the format is that you need two quarterbacks scoring. So we asked the obvious question: has anyone ever won a Tacos Tolteca matchup while one of their two QB slots scored 0.00, or was completely empty?

Yahoo only keeps this league's history back to 2024, so that's the window. We checked every matchup page for 2024, 2025, and 2026 through Week 9. The numbers below are Yahoo's matchup-page points.

The answer: 11 times. Five in 2024, five in 2025, and one so far in 2026. A twelfth is warming up on Monday night.

Greatest Hits

The empty-slot special. Baker's 4th Qtr ZYNers didn't even bother filling the second QB. Drake Maye put up 22.98 by himself, and that was enough to beat 2019 Dream Team 120.68–113.64. One QB. One win. Zero shame.

Back-to-back Breakers. Chase T-Law Breakers did it in Week 10 and Week 11 of 2024. Trevor Lawrence at 0.00 one week, Desmond Ridder at 0.00 the next. Flacco and Geno covered the bills. The team name promised Law, the lineup delivered vibes.

The Wilson/Fields three-peat. I would've hit the green turned a dead QB into a lifestyle in 2025. Three wins with a zero in one of the QB slots (Weeks 8, 11, and 13), all with the Russell Wilson / Justin Fields pairing. When Fields went dark in Week 13, Kirk Cousins stepped in for 15.36 and they still won by nearly 40. That's not a bug. That's a brand.

The 0.06-point miracle. Iced Goffee with GOAT Milk beat At least I higgins the green 138.26–138.20 in Week 16 of 2025 with Patrick Mahomes at 0.00. Jared Goff's 34.46 carried the whole operation by six hundredths of a point. Somewhere, a manager is still refreshing that page.

The IR special (2026). GettinGoff on Teep beat Painted Nails 104.94–99.08 in Week 8 with Lamar Jackson at 0.00 from the QB slot while on IR. Jared Goff scored just 12.04. The lowest winning score of that week, and still a W with a quarterback on injured reserve in the lineup. The bar is on the floor, and EQ cleared it.

Pending: Likely No. 12

Heading into Monday night of 2026 Week 9, Two Robinson's One Hole leads Drake & Bake(r) 126.38–109.70 with Cam Ward on bye in a QB slot (Bo Nix scored 20.68). If it holds, that's entry No. 12. Not final until the lights go out in Minnesota.

Honorable Mentions: The Other Side of the Coin

Winning with one QB is a party trick. Losing with one (or zero) is a cautionary tale.

  • 2026 Week 8: Coker!? I barely know her (ctay, the guy who runs this site) lost to Herbie is ASS 120.22–119.44 with C.J. Stroud on bye in a QB slot. A 0.78-point loss. One healthy QB and 0.79 more points, and this column has a different ending. The desk stays neutral on ctay's games. The desk also notes that the site owner cannot edit that score.
  • 2025 Weeks 16 and 17: RIPPIN FARTS put up both QB slots at 0.00 (Tyrod Taylor and Jayden Daniels) and lost 174.68–87.90 and 107.32–48.90. That is not a dead slot. That is a closed restaurant.
  • 2024: Starvin for Marvin nutz lost three times with an empty QB slot or a QB at 0.00 — Week 5 (empty, lost 91.80–118.82), Week 11 (empty, lost 124.00–161.08), and Week 16 (Jameis Winston 0.00, the same week Air Jayden won with Fields at zero). Consistency!
  • 2024 Week 10: 2019 Dream Team started Cooper Rush, who scored −2.00. Negative points from a QB slot. The Hall of Shame has a basement, and Rush found it.

The Takeaway

Eleven wins. One empty slot. Three seasons. Two managers who made it a personality trait. One site owner who almost made the winners list from the wrong direction.

Fill both QB slots. Or don't — and hope your other guy goes nuclear, your opponent also forgets how 2-QB works, and the scoreboard is feeling generous. The archive is watching.
