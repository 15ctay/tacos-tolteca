Broke by Week 3: How the Commish Blew $100 of FAB and Bought Himself a Bench Full of Backup QBs

Broke by Week 3: How the Commish Blew $100 of FAB and Bought Himself a Bench Full of Backup QBs

19 moves. Six quarterbacks streamed. $77 on Marcus Mariota. $0 left by September 23. Ima Burrow Into Polly set the (unofficial, made-up, but spiritually true) DGFFL record for running out of FAB, and then he started winning anyway.

Editor's note: This is a roast, and it's all love. Every number comes from Yahoo. The "record" is league lore. Nobody checked the record books, because nobody else has ever been broke this early.

Timeline of Financial Ruin

Every move ABMCommissioner made from Sep 10 to Oct 10, individually roasted, oldest first, because a disaster this big deserves to be enjoyed in order.

Sep 10: The QB carousel starts spinning

  • Added Jameis Winston (FA), dropped J.J. McCarthy. Day one of the waiver season, and the commish's first move is swapping one QB for another. In a 2QB league that's survival. In this case it was foreshadowing. Winston would last six days.

Sep 11

  • Added Cooper Rush (FA), dropped Mac Jones. Two days, two backup QBs. At this point he wasn't building a roster, he was collecting them like trading cards.

Sep 16: Waiver Wednesday, a.k.a. the beginning of the end

  • Carson Wentz, $13 waiver, dropped Jameis Winston. Winston got six days. Wentz got $13 and a bench spot he's still sitting on today. Thirteen dollars for a guy whose job is to wait.
  • Devaughn Vele, $9 waiver, dropped Rachaad White. Nine dollars on a WR. Hold that thought, because this one comes back later in a very different light.
  • Kalif Raymond, $1 waiver, dropped Cooper Rush. One dollar. Cooper Rush lasted five days, and Kalif Raymond would last exactly seven. A $1 rental. The FAB equivalent of feeding a parking meter you never come back to.
  • Joe Flacco (FA), 12:49 pm. Same day, the commish wakes up and remembers he still needs a QB. Free agent, no drop. Flacco is still on his bench too. QB count added so far: four.

Sep 23: The $77 Mariota Experience

  • Marcus Mariota, $77 waiver, dropped Jonathon Brooks (IR). Seventy-seven dollars. 77% of the season's budget on one player, in Week 3. Thirteen days later he'd be dropped with an "O" next to his name. That's roughly $5.92 a day to own Marcus Mariota. Premium cable is cheaper, and at least cable shows up.
  • Tank Bigsby, $0 waiver, dropped Kalif Raymond. And there goes the $1 man, after seven days. Bigsby joins for free, and stays exactly one week before landing on IR and getting cut.
  • Terrance Ferguson, $0 waiver, dropped Makai Lemon. Free TE, one week, then dropped from IR. Painted Nails picked him up for $0 on Oct 3. Even the leftovers had a second life somewhere else.
  • Dontayvion Wicks (FA), dropped Tyler Allgeier. Allgeier got picked up by Poconos Born Brock a week later. Wicks lasted until Oct 7. The commish doesn't drop players, he donates them.

Sep 30: The free aisle

  • Keenan Allen, $0 waiver, dropped Terrance Ferguson (IR). Out of money, so it's $0 bids from here on. Allen is still on the bench. Free real estate, and the commish moved right in.
  • Bears D, $0 waiver, dropped Eagles D. A defense for free. He'd trade it away for a QB eight days later, because of course he would.
  • Darren Waller (FA), dropped Tank Bigsby (IR). Waller was here for one week. Nobody got a chance to learn his name in the group chat.

Oct 3

  • Emanuel Wilson (FA), no drop. A Saturday-evening free agent. Mark this one down too, because it's going to look smart in about two sections.

Oct 6

  • Dropped Marcus Mariota (O). The $77 man, released to waivers. Thirteen days on the roster. The most expensive "it's not you, it's me" in league history.

Oct 7, 3:08 am: The witching hour

  • Will Shipley (FA), dropped Dontayvion Wicks. Nothing good happens on a fantasy app at 3am...
  • Dohnte Meyers (FA), dropped Darren Waller. ...and nothing good happens twice in the same minute either. Go to sleep, commish. The waiver wire will still be there in the morning.

Oct 8

  • Easton Stick (FA), dropped the Bears D. QB number six. Traded a free defense for a free QB.

Oct 10

  • Jets D (FA), dropped Easton Stick. Forty-one hours later, Stick is gone. Two days. Easton Stick had a shorter stay on this roster than most leftovers do in the fridge.

By the Numbers

  • Moves: 19 in 31 days (Sep 10 to Oct 10). Zero trades. The waiver wire has a reserved parking spot for this guy.
  • FAB spent: $100 of $100. FAB remaining: $0, last in the league.
  • The Mariota tax: $77 of $100, 77% of the budget, on a QB who lasted 13 days.
  • Where the rest went: Wentz $13, Vele $9, Raymond $1. Every other claim was $0 or a free agent.
  • Burn rate: $100 gone by Sep 23, Week 3. Over the four completed weeks, that's $25 a week.
  • QBs added: six (Winston, Rush, Wentz, Flacco, Mariota, Stick). Throw in the two he dropped on the way in (McCarthy, Mac Jones) and eight QBs have passed through this roster in a month.
  • League comparison: every other team still has money. The next-lowest, Painted Nails, has $4. The other 11 teams average about $66 left. Two Robinson's One Hole has all $100 and is 4-0, which means the league leader has spent exactly $100 less than the commish.

But Credit Where It's Due

Okay. We've had our fun. Now for the part where we admit something uncomfortable: it might be working.

Ima Burrow Into Polly opened 0-2, including the biggest blowout of Week 1 (102.26 to 177.58). Then the scores started going up, every single week: 102.26, 111.76, 141.06, 174.54. In Week 4 he posted 174.54, the top score of the week, beat Yahoo's projection by 43.02 and took down 2 hand my shit by 23.86. Two straight wins. He's 2-2, with 529.62 PF, sitting 7th, just a tiebreaker outside the playoff line in a league where five teams are 2-2.

And look at the pickups that stuck. Devaughn Vele, the $9 claim, is in his starting lineup. Emanuel Wilson, the Saturday-night free agent, is starting at flex. Keenan Allen cost $0, and Wentz, Flacco and Will Shipley give him real depth in a 2QB league behind Joe Burrow and Brock Purdy. The commish didn't waste the money so much as spend it all on a roster that's finally clicking.

Nobody in this league works harder. Nineteen moves, all at the waiver wire, and the team keeps getting better. As of Saturday night he's down just 14.20 to 23.54 against Taylor's world, but he's projected for 130.00 to 114.78. A third straight win would put him right in the middle of the playoff race.

Outlook: sunny. The wallet's empty, but the roster isn't, and a hot streak doesn't cost a dime. Don't be surprised if the broke commish is cashing a playoff check in January.
