Welcome to the Tacos Tolteca DGFFL site

The DGFFL has a website, and it's served fresh

Twelve teams. Two quarterbacks each. Six points per passing TD. Welcome to the official unofficial home of the Tacos Tolteca DGFFL, 2026 season.

What's on the menu:

  • Standings: W-L-T, points for and against, streaks, and how much FAB everyone has left. The gold dashed line marks the playoff cut (top 6).
  • Results: every week, every score, Yahoo's original projection under each final score, plus the top score and biggest blowout of each week.
  • Power Rankings: our formula blends record, points for and recent form. Take it up with the math.
  • Teams: a page for every squad, with roster by slot, game log, FAB left and recent moves.
  • News: recaps, previews and the occasional hot take.
  • Transactions: every add and drop, with a search box for anyone keeping tabs on their rivals.
  • League Info: full PPR, 2QB, 6-pt passing TDs, $100 FAB, keepers, playoffs Weeks 15–17 and a trade deadline of November 28, 2026.

Every number comes straight from Yahoo Fantasy. No made-up stats, just fresh ingredients.
