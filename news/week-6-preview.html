Week 6 Preview: four byes, a Kyle Allen start and the closest projected game of the week

Week 6 Preview

Four NFL teams are on bye this week: Cincinnati, Detroit, Miami and Minnesota. In a 2QB league that hurts, and several lineups were still showing bye-week players as of Tuesday afternoon (about 12:55pm ET). Projections are Yahoo's as of then, and every lineup can still change before kickoff. Thursday night is SEA @ DEN.

Ima Burrow Into Polly (proj 100.33) vs Drake & Bake(r) (proj 154.97)

The biggest projected gap of the week, 54.64. With Joe Burrow, Joe Flacco and Carson Wentz all on bye, ABMCommissioner is starting Brock Purdy and Kyle Allen, the QB he picked up Monday morning. The commish has won three straight on $0 FAB. Anthony has the week's top projection and the league's points lead, and he's coming off a loss in which he scored 166.84. Somebody is going to pay for that, and right now it looks like the commish. One thing for Anthony to check: his starting DEF is the Vikings, who are on bye.

The closest projected game of the week, 2.16, between the two 1-4 teams. ctay has the fourth-most points in the league. Benedetto has the fewest. One of them gets to 2-4. Bye-week notes on both sides: 2 hand my shit has Jahmyr Gibbs (DET) on the bench and Saquon Barkley (listed O) in a starting slot, and Likely to drop my IPA on you is starting Jameson Williams (DET) with Justin Jefferson (MIN) on the bench.
