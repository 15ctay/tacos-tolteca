From Penthouse to Outhouse: Herbie is ASS Is Finally Living Up to Its Name

Last year's king is 1-3 and in 11th place. A team named after an ass, run by a manager who's scared to eat it. A DGFFL roast.

Editor's note: This is a roast. It's all love, and it's all about fantasy football. Every 2026 number below comes straight from Yahoo. The part about last season is league lore, and Lem can argue with it in the group chat.

The Fall

Legend has it that last season lem0324 finished in first place. We don't have last year's numbers on this site, so we'll leave that to the oral historians. What we do know: a champion sat atop the DGFFL, gazing down on the peasants.

Then he named his team Herbie is ASS. Then he started Justin Herbert at QB anyway. Then the team took the name personally.

Four weeks into 2026, the former king is 1-3, 11th of 12, riding an L-2 streak. He's out of the playoff spots and just one spot above the basement.

And here's the running theme of this column: Lem put "ASS" right there on the menu, in all caps, in his team name, and still won't order it. Four weeks of staring at it. Not one bite. The man is afraid to eat ass, and his lineup knows it.

By the Numbers

  • Record: 1-3-0. Rank: 11th.
  • Points for: 464.70, 11th in the league. The only team with fewer is Bands Emeka Dance (463.46), so Lem is ahead of last place by 1.24 points. That's not a cushion, that's a coaster.
  • Points against: 484.72, the third-fewest in the league. So no, Lem, the schedule is not the problem. Opponents have been handing you a beautiful, generous platter, and you've been too shy to dig in.
  • Projections vs. reality: Yahoo projected Herbie is ASS for 511.85 points over four weeks. It scored 464.70. That's 47.15 points left on the table, uneaten.
  • FAB left: $56, and zero Herbie is ASS moves among the league's 25 most recent transactions. The waiver wire is a buffet, and Lem's been sitting at the table with his hands folded.

Exhibit A-SS: The Game Log

Week 1: L, 94.86 to 107.88 (−13.02) vs. Two Allens One Endzone. Yahoo projected 134.44. Lem delivered 94.86, the lowest score of the whole season, by anyone, in any week. He missed his projection by 39.58, the worst miss vs. projection by any team all season. What a debut.

Week 2: W, 127.06 to 98.22 (+28.84) vs. Bands Emeka Dance. The lone win, and it came against the only team that has scored fewer points than he has. Enjoy it, Lem.

Week 3: L, 106.14 to 141.06 (−34.92) vs. Ima Burrow Into Polly. Projected for 116.47, which was already a low bar. He scored 106.14 and lost by nearly 35. Ima Burrow Into Polly ate. Lem watched.

Week 4: L, 136.64 to 137.56 (−0.92) vs. GettinGoff on CTay. His best score of the year, 8.86 over projection, and he still lost by 0.92, the closest game of the season. Somewhere on that bench was a point. One point. He had it right there, on a plate, and he didn't take a bite. Afraid, again.

The Roster: A Crime Scene in Two QBs

This is a 2QB, 6-point-passing-TD league, and Lem's quarterbacks are Justin Herbert and Matthew Stafford. Read the team name again. He named the franchise after his own starting QB and then called him an ass. That's not trash talk, that's a self-own with a logo.

The rest of the starting lineup: Quinshon Judkins and Braelon Allen at RB, George Pickens and Christian Watson at WR, Mark Andrews at TE, TreVeyon Henderson at flex, and the Texans D. On the bench, Tetairoa McMillan, Malik Willis, Jacory Croskey-Merritt, Keaton Mitchell, Malik Washington and Ollie Gordon II are all waiting for a coach brave enough to make a change.

No trades. No adds. No drops in the recent wire. Just a man, his $56 and a deep fear of anything labeled "ASS."

Week 5: Is This the Week He Finally Eats?

There might be a twist. As of Saturday night, Herbie is ASS leads Likely to drop my IPA on you 28.00 to 0.00, with a 141.63 projection to Benedetto's 115.52. Only Drake & Bake(r) (159.04), Poconos Born Brock (151.57) and Bands Emeka Dance (142.17) are projected higher.

So the platter is ready and the forks are out. All he has to do is not freeze up at the table. Again.

The Path Back to Respectability: A Prescription

  1. Spend the $56. Fifty-six FAB dollars are no use to anyone sitting in the wallet.
  2. Pick a lane on the name. Either Herbie is ASS, or Herbie is your QB1. Right now he's both, and that's how you go 1-3.
  3. Find the 0.92. Check the bench before lineups lock. That one point is still out there somewhere.
  4. Beat a projection. He's done it once in four tries. Twice would be a trend.
  5. Get over the fear. It's on the menu. You ordered it when you named your team. Eat the ass, Lem. Metaphorically. Fantasy-wise. You know what we mean.
