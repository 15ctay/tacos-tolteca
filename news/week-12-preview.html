Week 12 Preview: Thanksgiving Week Brings No Byes, Six Week 1 Rematches, and a Saturday Trade Deadline

Week 12 Preview

Thanksgiving week, and no NFL team is on bye. Every starter on every roster has a game. Projections and lineups are Yahoo's as of Tuesday afternoon (about 12:45pm ET), and every lineup can still change before kickoff. Remember the Thursday games: set your lineup before the turkey.

Two more things. The trade deadline is Saturday, Nov 28, and trades still need the commish's two-day review. And all six games this week are rematches of Week 1. The Week 1 results are below, under the team names from back then.

Coker!? I barely know her (proj 140.17) vs Painted Nails (proj 134.91)

Yahoo has ctay by 5.26. In Week 1, back when ctay's team was "2 hand my shit," Antonio won 175.96 to 174.22, a 1.74-point game. Both teams have won three straight, so one streak ends Sunday. Antonio has no injury tags in his lineup. As always when ctay is playing, the desk makes no pick.
